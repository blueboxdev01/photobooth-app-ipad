# v0.12.2-ipad — The iPad display can actually connect

**Download `photobooth-v0.12.2-win-x64.zip`, unzip, run `Photobooth.Server.exe`.**
Then open <http://localhost:5000/operator>.

> The version at the foot of the rail reads **`0.12.2-ipad`**. Please quote it
> with any result.

## The fix

An iPad that had been set up correctly — certificate installed and trusted,
address right, Setup showing its fingerprint — would open the display, load the
page, and then sit on **"Connecting…"** for ever.

Everything visible was working. The booth served the page over HTTPS, so the
certificate and the network were plainly fine. **Only the live connection was
missing**, and nothing anywhere reported an error.

**What was wrong:** the guest screen gets its updates over a live channel, and
opening that channel takes two steps — a short handshake to agree how to
connect, and then the connection itself. The booth's network port allowed the
second and refused the first, so the display could never get past the handshake.
The allowlist had been tested against the channel's own address and never
against its handshake.

Both are allowed now, and nothing else was opened up: the operator console,
settings and the session controls are still refused on that port, verified by
test and by trying them.

## If you are on v0.12.1 or older

There is no workaround. Upgrade — the iPad display cannot connect on those
builds.

Guest photos over wifi are **unaffected** and work on v0.12.1. If your colleague
is mid-test on that feature, they can finish before taking this.

## A known limitation, not fixed here

**A custom backdrop set in Setup does not reach the iPad.** The guest screen
keeps the default dark background. The iPad reads its backdrop from a settings
endpoint that is deliberately refused on the network port — it carries folder
paths and account details that have no business on a guest network.

This fails quietly and harmlessly: the display works, it is just always the
default colour. A proper fix means giving the display a narrow endpoint of its
own carrying only the backdrop, which is a change worth making deliberately
rather than squeezing into a hotfix.

## Everything else

Unchanged from v0.12.1: guests download their own photos over your wifi, the
address picker, and the whole of v0.10.0 underneath.
