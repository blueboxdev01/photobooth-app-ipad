# v0.12.3-ipad — Sharp photos in the strip

**Download `photobooth-v0.12.3-win-x64.zip`, unzip, run `Photobooth.Server.exe`.**
Then open <http://localhost:5000/operator>.

> The version at the foot of the rail reads **`0.12.3-ipad`**. Please quote it
> with any result.

## The fix

Photos inside a finished strip looked soft and faintly crunchy beside the very
files they were built from. That was a bug, not the cost of the photos being
smaller on the strip.

Every photo was being drawn with **nearest-neighbour sampling**. A 6000×4000
camera frame landing in a 560×420 slot is close to a **tenfold reduction**, and
point sampling keeps roughly one source pixel in ninety and averages nothing at
all. Fine detail does not soften under that — it breaks apart. Hair goes to
dotted lines, fabric texture turns to noise, and anything regular moirés.

Fixed by filtering properly on the way down, so every source pixel contributes.
**The frame art is fixed too** — art that is not an exact match for the canvas
was being point-sampled as well, which is worst of all on lettering and hard
edges.

Strips are also encoded at slightly higher JPEG quality (92 → 95).

## What to look at

This is the one thing in the build that needs a **real camera** to judge. The
mock camera's sample photos are only 1800×1200, so they never showed the problem
at anything like full strength.

1. Run a session with the **Canon at full resolution**, using your 6×4 landscape
   template and its frame art.
2. Open the finished strip next to one of the individual photos from the same
   session, both at 100%.
3. Look at **hair, eyebrows, fabric texture and the edge of a shirt** — that is
   where point sampling showed most.
4. Look at the **frame art's lettering and any fine line work** for stair-stepping.

**What to expect, honestly:** the strip photo still holds less detail than the
original, because its slot is physically smaller and no amount of filtering
changes that. What should be gone is the crunchiness — it should read as a
smaller photo, not a damaged one.

If it still looks wrong, say so and send the strip. That is a useful result.

## Everything else

Unchanged from v0.12.2: guests download their own photos over your wifi, the
address picker, the iPad guest display, and the whole of v0.10.0 underneath.

The known limitation from v0.12.2 still stands: a custom backdrop set in Setup
does not reach the iPad, which keeps its default dark background.
